# Original Equations

## 1. Power of a function

$$
\frac{d}{dx}g(y)^m=mg(y)^{m-1}\cdot g'(y)y'
$$

---

## 2. Cubic function

$$
\frac{d}{dx}f(u)^3=3f(u)^2\cdot f'(u)u'
$$

---

## 3. Negative power

$$
\frac{d}{dx}h(t)^{-2}=-2h(t)^{-3}\cdot h'(t)t'
$$

---

## 4. Fractional power

$$
\frac{d}{dx}p(z)^{\frac{1}{2}}
=
\frac{1}{2}p(z)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\cdot p'(z)z'
$$

---

## 5. Sine function

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\sin(f(y))
=
\cos(f(y))\cdot f'(y)y'
$$

---

## 6. Cosine function

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\cos(g(t))
=
-\sin(g(t))\cdot g'(t)t'
$$

---

## 7. Exponential function

$$
\frac{d}{dx}e^{f(u)}
=
e^{f(u)}\cdot f'(u)u'
$$

---

## 8. Natural logarithm

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\ln(h(y))
=
\frac{1}{h(y)}\cdot h'(y)y'
$$

---

## 9. Square root

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\sqrt{f(t)}
=
\frac{1}{2\sqrt{f(t)}}\cdot f'(t)t'
$$

---

## 10. Nested chain rule

$$
\frac{d}{dx}f(g(y))
=
f'(g(y))\cdot g'(y)y'
$$
---
